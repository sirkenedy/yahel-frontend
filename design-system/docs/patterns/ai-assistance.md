# AI assistance

Applies to any feature that generates, summarises, suggests or acts on the person's behalf: drafting mail, summarising a thread, suggesting meeting times, formulas, search answers. No such feature is in scope for Phase 1; these rules exist so the first one is built right.

## Rules

- **The person stays in control.** Generated content is a suggestion until the person accepts it. Nothing is sent, shared, deleted or paid for without an explicit action. Anything hard to undo is proposed with its effect stated, then confirmed by the person; destructive actions are never automated.
- **Always labelled.** Generated content is marked as such where it appears, with a consistent icon and words, not colour alone.
- **Works without it.** Every task can be completed with assistance turned off, unavailable or declined. No core flow depends on a generated result.
- **Easy to refine and undo.** Offer retry, edit and discard. Accepting can be undone like any other edit. When the person edits, rejects or rates a result, show that it took effect.
- **Show progress.** While generating, show that work is happening and allow cancelling. Say what is actually being done ("Summarising 12 messages"), not "Processing". Streamed text does not move content the person is reading.
- **Show sources.** Summaries and answers link to the messages, files or events they came from, limited to what the person can access.
- **Say what it cannot do.** State limits and the possibility of mistakes in plain words at the point of use, once, not as a wall of disclaimers.
- **Guide the request.** An open prompt offers a few example requests that show what is possible.
- **When it cannot help.** A blocked or failed request says so plainly, without blame, and suggests a request that would work.
- **People are not guessed.** Generated text and images never assume a person's gender, age or background. Ask, or stay neutral.
- **Respect context and privacy.** Assistance works within the current account context. It never draws on organisation content for a personal task or the reverse. People and administrators can see and control what data is used, and whether it is used at all. Personal content is used to improve a feature only after explicit agreement (the person's, or for an organisation its administrator's), which can be withdrawn.
- **Never impersonate.** Suggested replies and drafts are clearly drafts; the person is the author only after they accept. Nobody is led to believe that generated content or an automated reply came from a person.
- **Feedback.** A simple way to mark a result as good or bad, with an optional reason.
- **Accessible.** Announced to screen readers when ready, operable by keyboard, no reliance on animation to signal activity.

## Placement

Assistance appears where the work is (in the compose field, beside the document) as an optional control, not as a separate destination and not as an interruption. It uses the standard components and the single accent; it does not get its own colour or visual language.
