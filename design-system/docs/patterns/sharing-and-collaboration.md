# Sharing and collaboration

Sharing is the most consequential everyday action in a workspace. It must be easy to do right and hard to do by accident.

## Sharing

- **Share** is in the toolbar of every shareable item, in the same position, with the same icon.
- The share view answers three questions in order: who has access now, who to add, and what they can do.
- **Access in words.** A one-line summary at the top: "Only you", "Only invited people can edit", "Anyone at Northwind Studio with the link can view".
- **Roles** are the same across apps: can view, can comment, can edit, owner. Never more than these without a strong reason.
- **Least access by default.** New shares default to view; links default to restricted.
- **Crossing a boundary is explicit.** Adding someone outside the organisation, or making a link public, shows a clear notice and the person's external status before confirming. Organisation policy that prevents it is explained, not just disabled.
- **Preview the effect.** Changing a folder's access says how many items are affected.
- **Notify with intent.** The sharer chooses whether to send a message; the notification says who shared what and at which role.
- **Stopping sharing** is as easy as starting. Removing access takes effect immediately and says so.

## Markers

- A shared item carries a shared marker in every list; a public link a distinct one.
- External people are labelled wherever their name or avatar appears.
- Items show their owner and context (personal or which organisation).

## Working together

- **Presence.** Avatars of people currently in the document sit next to Share; each person has a stable colour, plus their name on their cursor and selection, so colour is never the only identifier.
- **Changes** by others appear without stealing focus or moving the person's cursor or scroll position.
- **Comments and suggestions** are attached to the content they refer to, show author and time, can be resolved, and notify the people involved.
- **Mentions** offer only people who can be given access, and offer to share if the mentioned person lacks it.
- **Version history** shows who changed what and when, and any version can be restored without losing the current one.
- **Requests for access** go to the owner with one-step approve or decline.

## Meet, Chat and Calendar

- Joining shows who is already there and whether the call is being recorded before the person enters.
- Guests and external participants are labelled.
- Invitations show the organiser, time in the viewer's time zone, and what responding will share.
