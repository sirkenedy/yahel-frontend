Design brief · Sign-in and onboarding

Daylight.

The first five minutes with Yahel should feel like walking into a bright, quiet room: one question at a time, nothing hidden, nothing to fear. Match Google’s proven flow step for step, then beat it on clarity, speed and trust. No images. The light is the design.

01One question per screenEvery screen asks one thing and shows how much is left (“03 / 07 · About 90 sec”). Google asks the same questions; we tell you why and how long.
02The account builds in front of youA receipt on the right fills in as you answer. No surprises at the end, and every line can be edited on Review.
03Passkeys first, passwords as fallbackFingerprint or face by default; admins must use one. Faster than a password and it can’t be phished.
04Your data stays in AfricaSaid once, plainly, where it matters: in the footer, on the account summary and at the finish. No settings to manage, no fine print.
05Keyboard first, mouse optionalEnter always advances. Codes paste into six boxes. Addresses check as you type, with free alternatives one tap away.
06Plain words, no fearSay what happens and why: “It can’t be changed later.” “No one else sees it.” No jargon, no security theatre, no images.
07Business without a help articleOne-click domain verification with your host, a TXT record only as fallback, and a diagnosis when it fails instead of “try again”.
08Light, not decorationPaper canvas, one indigo action per screen, Bricolage headlines, generous space. The brand mark appears twice: top-left and at the finish.
The prompt

“Design sign-in and account creation for a workspace that promises Africa’s data stays in Africa. Keep Google’s order of steps. Remove every moment of doubt: show progress and time left, show the account forming, default to passkeys, say plainly that the data stays in Africa, and fix domain problems for the user instead of describing them. Use no imagery: paper, ink and one indigo. Every screen must work with only a keyboard and read clearly to a screen reader.”

House rules
  • —One primary button, always indigo, always bottom-left of the question
  • —Headline ≤ 6 words, ends with a full stop or question mark
  • —Inputs 56px, radius 14, focus = 2px indigo + soft ring
  • —Motion: 520ms ease-out rise, 50ms stagger, off when reduced-motion is set
  • —Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 everywhere; never colour alone for state
  • —Placeholders in [BRACKETS] until the business confirms them
