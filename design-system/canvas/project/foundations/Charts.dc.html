Bars, lines and stacks may use all eight. Scatter plots and maps, where any two colours can touch, are capped at three: blue, amber and red.
Rules
Form first. One number is a figure, not a chart. Never two y-axes.
Colour follows the entity. Filtering never repaints what remains.
Legend plus direct labels. Always a legend for two or more series; label up to four directly.
Recessive scaffolding. Few grid lines, quiet axes, bars from zero.
A table behind every chart. Same data, real table, plus a text summary.
Status colours are reserved. Red, orange and green states are never "series 4".
